          <h2>The challenge</h2>
<h5>Generative AI and Agentic AI are introducing new opportunities—and new risks. Prioritizing secure and agentic AI accelerates adoption and builds trust.</h5>
<p><a data-barba-transition="true" href="https://www.accenture.com/en/insights/generative-ai" class="cmp-text__link cmp-text__link--default" target="_self" rel="noopener noreferrer" data-cmp-clickable="" data-cmp-data-layer="{&quot;block-the-challenge-link-1&quot;:{&quot;analytics-link-name&quot;:&quot;generative ai&quot;,&quot;analytics-link-type&quot;:&quot;engagement&quot;,&quot;xdm:linkURL&quot;:&quot;https://www.accenture.com/en/insights/generative-ai&quot;,&quot;analytics-engagement&quot;:&quot;true&quot;}}">Generative AI</a> and Agentic AI have introduced a new era of enterprise intelligence that organizations must embrace, or risk falling behind. However, it also elevates cybersecurity risks and introduces new vulnerabilities, which requires embedding security and trust by design into any AI program. And while most CEOs see cybersecurity as a major consideration for gen AI, only 6% feel they have a robust <a data-barba-transition="true" href="https://www.accenture.com/en/services/ai-data/responsible-ai" class="cmp-text__link cmp-text__link--default" target="_self" rel="noopener noreferrer" data-cmp-clickable="" data-cmp-data-layer="{&quot;block-the-challenge-link-2&quot;:{&quot;analytics-link-name&quot;:&quot;responsible ai&quot;,&quot;analytics-link-type&quot;:&quot;engagement&quot;,&quot;xdm:linkURL&quot;:&quot;https://www.accenture.com/en/services/ai-data/responsible-ai&quot;,&quot;analytics-engagement&quot;:&quot;true&quot;}}">responsible AI</a> foundation. Accenture can help close that gap and establish resiliency across your AI lifecycle.</p>
