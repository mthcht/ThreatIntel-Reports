<h3 id="a-dangerous-bird-with-odd-plumage">A Dangerous Bird with Odd Plumage</h3>
<figure><img src="/images/legacy/sysinturla/71d5ad11-cassowary.jpg" alt="Cassowary â âThe Worldâs Most Dangerous Birdâ" loading="lazy" width="684" height="480"><figcaption>Cassowary â âThe Worldâs Most Dangerous Birdâ</figcaption></figure>
<p>In 2017, Brandon Levene, Robert Falcone, and Tyler Halfpop of Palo Alto networks <a href="https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/unit42-kazuar-multiplatform-espionage-backdoor-api-access/">discovered</a> a new toolkit they called âKazuarâ. <p>Newer Kazuar samples are (poorly) branded to look like a <a href="https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/downloads/debugview">SysInternal tool called âDebugViewâ</a>.
