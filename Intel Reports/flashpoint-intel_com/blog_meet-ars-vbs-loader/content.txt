			</div><div class="glossary-items-container"><div class="glossary-items-container__group open" id="a"><div class="group-divider"><div class="letter">a</div><div class="letter-arrow"><img decoding="async" src="https://flashpoint.io/wp-content/themes/flashpoint/block-icons/angle-down-purple.svg" /></div></div><div class="group-content"><div class="left">a</div><div class="right"><div class="glossary-item"><a href="https://flashpoint.io/intelligence-101/advanced-persistent-threat/"><h5>Advanced persistent threat</h5><p>A highly motivated threat actor or threat actor group, usually sponsored by a nation-state. Advanced persistent threats (APTs) often aim to gain undetected access to a network and then remain silently persistent, establish a backdoor, and/or steal data, as opposed to causing damage. Once inside the target network, APTs leverage malware to achieve their directives, which may include acquiring and exfiltrating data. APT groups are identified through several different naming conventions.</p><div class="read">Read More →</div></a></div><div class="glossary-item"><a href="https://flashpoint.io/intelligence-101/ai-threat-intelligence/"><h5>AI Threat Intelligence</h5><p>AI in threat intelligence refers to the use of artificial intelligence technologies and techniques to improve the detection, analysis, and response to cyber threats.</p><div class="read">Read More →</div></a></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Alphabay</h5><p>A darknet marketplace, originally launched in September 2014, that has been considered one of the most popular and comprehensive illicit marketplaces to exist. The original AlphaBay was taken down by law enforcement’s “Operation Bayonet” in July 2017, and was relaunched by one of its original admins in August 2021. Its most popular offerings include drugs, fraud-related listings like credit cards and fullz, and guides and tutorials for fraud, hacking, and social engineering. </p></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Anonymous</h5><p>Anonymous is a globally recognized decentralized hacktivist collective, known for its widespread cyberattacks targeting numerous governments, government institutions, government agencies, and corporations. These actions have led to the arrest of numerous individuals involved in Anonymous cyberattacks across various countries, including the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, the Netherlands, South Africa, Spain, India, and Turkey.

Although Anonymous&#8217; media presence declined by 2018, the group resurfaced in 2020 to provide support for the George Floyd protests and other social causes. The decentralized structure of Anonymous enables different hackers in private chat rooms to contribute to different operations, while also allowing individuals who align with their mission to adopt the &#8220;Anonymous&#8221; label, regardless of formal group affiliation.</p></div><div class="glossary-item"><a href="https://flashpoint.io/intelligence-101/artificial-intelligence/"><h5>Artificial Intelligence (AI)</h5><p>Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the field of computer science focused on developing intelligent machines capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence. It involves the creation and implementation of algorithms and systems that enable machines to perceive, reason, learn, and make decisions based on data and patterns.</p><div class="read">Read More →</div></a></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Attack surface</h5><p>The attack surface refers to the sum of all the potential points of vulnerability in a system, application, or network that an attacker can exploit. It encompasses both the known and unknown vulnerabilities, including entry points, weak configurations, exposed services, and any other potential avenues for unauthorized access or compromise.</p></div></div></div></div><div class="glossary-items-container__group" id="b"><div class="group-divider"><div class="letter">b</div><div class="letter-arrow"><img decoding="async" src="https://flashpoint.io/wp-content/themes/flashpoint/block-icons/angle-down-purple.svg" /></div></div><div class="group-content"><div class="left">b</div><div class="right"><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Bitcoin</h5><p>A digital currency that uses cryptology to secure transactions and control the creation of additional currencies. Bitcoin is regarded as the first decentralized cryptocurrency and is the most widely used by cybercriminals. Its currency abbreviation is BTC.</p></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Botnet</h5><p>A network of computers controlled by malware and used for malicious purposes.</p></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Brand impersonation</h5><p>Brand impersonation refers to the act of creating fake online identities, websites, or social media accounts that mimic a legitimate brand or organization. The impersonators use these deceptive tactics to deceive and defraud individuals, often to gain access to sensitive information or to scam unsuspecting users.</p></div><div class="glossary-item"><a href="https://flashpoint.io/intelligence-101/breach-forums/"><h5>Breach Forums</h5><p>BreachForums was a prominent English-language illicit cybercriminal forum established on March 16, 2022. Created in the wake of the seizure of RaidForums, it quickly became the primary underground marketplace for the trade, sale, and distribution of compromised datasets and stolen information.</p><div class="read">Read More →</div></a></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Business email compromise</h5><p>BEC—a type of email fraud that usually leads to payment fraud or the obtaining of sensitive corporate information.</p></div></div></div></div><div class="glossary-items-container__group" id="c"><div class="group-divider"><div class="letter">c</div><div class="letter-arrow"><img decoding="async" src="https://flashpoint.io/wp-content/themes/flashpoint/block-icons/angle-down-purple.svg" /></div></div><div class="group-content"><div class="left">c</div><div class="right"><div class="glossary-item"><a href="https://flashpoint.io/intelligence-101/cisa/"><h5>CISA</h5><p>The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) is a U.S. government agency responsible for protecting and securing the nation&#8217;s critical infrastructure from cyber threats, coordinating cybersecurity efforts across federal, state, and local governments, and providing support and resources to enhance cybersecurity resilience.</p><div class="read">Read More →</div></a></div><div class="glossary-item"><a href="https://flashpoint.io/intelligence-101/clop/"><h5>Clop</h5><p>Clop (also known as Cl0p) is an extortionist ransomware-type malware that originated in 2019 and operates on the Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS) model. It belongs to the CryptoMix ransomware family and has undergone multiple iterations and advancements since its initial appearance. In May 2023, gained massive attention by exploiting the MOVEit vulnerability, resulting in a series of damaging cyberattacks impacting nearly 100 victims worldwide, many of which have come public.</p><div class="read">Read More →</div></a></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Compromised credentials</h5><p>Compromised credentials refer to login information (e.g., username and password) that has been illicitly obtained by unauthorized individuals or cyber attackers, potentially leading to unauthorized access and misuse of the associated online accounts or systems. This security breach can result from various methods, such as hacking, phishing, or malware attacks.</p></div><div class="glossary-item"><a href="https://flashpoint.io/intelligence-101/continuous-threat-exposure-management/"><h5>Continuous Threat Exposure Management</h5><p>Learn more about continuous threat exposure management (CTEM) and how Flashpoint intelligence can accelerate your processes.</p><div class="read">Read More →</div></a></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Corporate security</h5><p>Corporate security refers to the strategies, protocols, and measures put in place by organizations to protect their physical assets, personnel, and sensitive information from internal and external threats. It encompasses a range of practices and disciplines aimed at maintaining a secure and safe environment for employees, visitors, and the organization as a whole.</p></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Credential stuffing</h5><p>Automatically entering a large number of credentials (usually obtained from data breaches) into websites until they are matched to an existing account. Used in account takeover attacks.</p></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Crypting</h5><p>Crypting is the process of encrypting or obfuscating malicious code to evade detection by security software and analysts. </p></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>CVE (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures)</h5><p>CVE stands for &#8220;Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures.&#8221; It is a system used by organizations and researchers to track and discuss publicly known vulnerabilities in various software and hardware products.</p></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>CVSS</h5><p>The Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS) is a standardized framework used to assess and quantify the severity of security vulnerabilities in computer systems or software. CVSS assigns scores based on various metrics to help organizations prioritize and address security risks effectively.</p></div><div class="glossary-item"><a href="https://flashpoint.io/intelligence-101/cyber-threat-intelligence/"><h5>Cyber Threat Intelligence</h5><p>Often referred to by the acronym CTI, Cyber Threat Intelligence refers to the information, data, and context that’s used to detect, assess, prioritize, and counter cyber threats in order to prevent potential attacks against an organization and reduce risk. Although the terms Cyber Threat intelligence and Threat Intelligence (CTI) are sometimes used interchangeably, they do have distinct nuances: Cyber Threat Intelligence specifically focuses on cyber threats, even those they may overlap with or manifest in the physical work, whereas Threat intelligence encompasses a broad spectrum of risks, including physical, geopolitical, and cyber threats. </p><div class="read">Read More →</div></a></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Cyberattack</h5><p>A cyberattack is a deliberate and malicious act carried out by threat actors to compromise the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of computer systems, networks, or data. Cyberattacks encompass various techniques and tactics, such as exploiting vulnerabilities, ransomware, and DDoS attacks.</p></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Cybersecurity</h5><p>Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computer systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access, breaches, and attacks. It involves implementing measures, processes, and technologies to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of digital information, safeguarding against potential threats and vulnerabilities in the digital landscape.</p></div></div></div></div><div class="glossary-items-container__group" id="d"><div class="group-divider"><div class="letter">d</div><div class="letter-arrow"><img decoding="async" src="https://flashpoint.io/wp-content/themes/flashpoint/block-icons/angle-down-purple.svg" /></div></div><div class="group-content"><div class="left">d</div><div class="right"><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Dark web</h5><p>The part of the internet that is accessible only through special software, such as Tor, which includes security and obfuscation measures to preserve users&#8217; and website operators&#8217; anonymity.</p></div><div class="glossary-item"><h5>Darknet</h5><p>Also known as the dark web, the darknet is a hidden part of the internet that requires specific software, configurations, or authorization to access. It enables users to operate on encrypted networks, offering anonymity and privacy.</p></div><div class="glossary-item"><a href="https://flashpoint.io/intelligence-101/data-breach/"><h5>Data breach</h5><p>A data breach occurs whenever sensitive data is unintentionally exposed.
