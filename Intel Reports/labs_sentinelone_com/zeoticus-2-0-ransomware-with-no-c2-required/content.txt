class="article-entry-main"><div class="text-rich-longform text-b3-body-400 ui-text-secondary"><h2 id="overview">Overview</h2><p>Zeoticus ransomware first appeared for sale in various underground forums and markets in early 2020. Initially, the ransomware was offered as a complete custom build for an undisclosed fee. The ransomware is currently Windows-specific and, according to the developers, functions on all “supported versions of Windows”. </p><p>Unusually, there are no connectivity requirements for the payloads to execute. Zeoticus ransomware will execute fully offline, with no dependence on a C2 (Command &amp; Control). It is also worth noting that the malware is designed not to function in some regions, specifically Russia, Belarus, and Kyrgyzstan. Like many other families, use within the <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Independent_States">CIS</a> is discouraged in order to avoid any backlash from regional government and law enforcement agencies.</p><p><img alt="" loading="lazy" width="800" height="450" decoding="async" data-nimg="1" class="rich-text-asset" style="color:transparent" sizes="(max-width: 768px) 100vw, 768px" srcSet="https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt53c99b43892c2378/blt81494eeb9efcb8f9/6aa3178aeb9443bf87949537/Zeoticus_orig_xss.jpg?width=375&amp;quality=90&amp;format=auto 375w, https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt53c99b43892c2378/blt81494eeb9efcb8f9/6aa3178aeb9443bf87949537/Zeoticus_orig_xss.jpg?width=768&amp;quality=90&amp;format=auto 768w, https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt53c99b43892c2378/blt81494eeb9efcb8f9/6aa3178aeb9443bf87949537/Zeoticus_orig_xss.jpg?width=1280&amp;quality=90&amp;format=auto 1280w, https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt53c99b43892c2378/blt81494eeb9efcb8f9/6aa3178aeb9443bf87949537/Zeoticus_orig_xss.jpg?width=1920&amp;quality=90&amp;format=auto 1920w, https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt53c99b43892c2378/blt81494eeb9efcb8f9/6aa3178aeb9443bf87949537/Zeoticus_orig_xss.jpg?width=2560&amp;quality=90&amp;format=auto 2560w" src="https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/blt53c99b43892c2378/blt81494eeb9efcb8f9/6aa3178aeb9443bf87949537/Zeoticus_orig_xss.jpg?width=2560&amp;quality=90&amp;format=auto"/></p><h2 id="zeoticus-development">Zeoticus Development</h2><p>Since late 2020 and moving into early 2021, the vendor has continued to maintain and offer updates on the Zeoticus service.</p><p>In December 2020, samples of Zeoticus 2.0 were observed and reported in the wild. We encourage all to review their security posture and take any necessary steps to improve their protections and reduce their overall exposure. Visibility and education go a long way here.
