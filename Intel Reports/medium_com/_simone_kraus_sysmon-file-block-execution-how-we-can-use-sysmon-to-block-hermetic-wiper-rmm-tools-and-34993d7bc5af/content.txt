Even if the companies have it, to what extent can the solutions be used in the ICS environment, and wouldn’t it be an effective backup in the ICS environment to have a Sysmon configuration with specific IDs that blocks malware, ransomware, malicious drivers or remote desktop tools in addition to backups?
