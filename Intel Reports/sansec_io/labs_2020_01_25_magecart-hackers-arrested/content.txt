on December 20th. The suspects are from Jakarta and Yogyakarta and are 23, 26 and 35 years old. After the press conference, one suspect admitted on <a href="https://www.metrotvnews.com/play/NleC6Ale-eksklusif-pengakuan-hacker-pembobol-situs-e-commerce">Indonesian television</a> that he had injected web skimmers into ecommerce stores since 2017 and only made enough money to &quot;buy a jacket&quot;. The suspects face up to 10 years in prison under article 363 of the Indonesian Criminal Code.</p><p>The Indonesian police reported that this group had intercepted payments for 12 (mostly European) stores.
