class="content_col"><div><h1 class="article_title">View Access Logs for your workspace</h1><div class="article_body">
<p>Access Logs are an easy way to check for any unusual or suspicious sign-in activity. On the Pro, Business+, and Enterprise Grid plans, owners and admins can use Access Logs to view sign-in details for all members of a workspace.</p>
<h3><strong>What can you see using Access Logs? </strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>The time and date of each new sign in</li>
<li>The IP address of each device that has accessed each account</li>
<li>A list of devices that have accessed each account</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="anchor">
<br><strong>View Access Logs for all members</strong>
</h2>
<div class="boxed">
<ol>
<li>Click your <strong>workspace name</strong> in the sidebar.<br><img class="localize_image_workspace_picker image--size-50 image--border-round" src="https://a.slack-edge.com/8e8e4/helpcenter/img/workspace-picker@2x.jpg">
</li>
<li>Hover over <strong>Tools &amp; settings</strong>, then click <strong>Workspace settings</strong>.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Access Logs</strong> on the right.</li>
<li>If you notice any irregularities, ask the affected member(s) to <strong>sign out of all sessions</strong> from their <a href="https://my.slack.com/account/logs">personal Access Logs</a>.</li>
</ol>
</div>
<p class="note"><strong>Note: </strong>Members can check their <a href="https://slack.com/help/articles/360002084827-View-Access-Logs-for-your-account">personal Access Logs</a> if they’re worried that their account has been compromised.</p>
<h2 class="anchor">
<br><strong>Understand Access Log entries</strong>
</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">When members are connected to and actively using Slack, workspace Access Logs are updated every hour. New entries are generated when members do any of the following: </span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Sign in to their account</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Open the Slack app on their computer or mobile device</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;">Sign in with a unique IP address and a desktop or mobile device</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;">Change their <a href="https://slack.com/help/articles/201864558-Set-your-Slack-status-and-availability" target="_self">availability</a> from away to active</li>
</ul>
<div class="boxed roles">
<strong>Who can use this feature?</strong>
<ul>
<li class="ts_icon ts_icon_user">
<strong>Workspace Owners</strong>/<strong>Admins</strong> and <strong>Org Owners</strong>/<strong>Admins</strong>
</li>
<li class="ts_icon ts_icon_flag">Available on <a href="https://slack.com/pricing" target="_self"><strong>paid plans</strong></a>
</li>
</ul>
