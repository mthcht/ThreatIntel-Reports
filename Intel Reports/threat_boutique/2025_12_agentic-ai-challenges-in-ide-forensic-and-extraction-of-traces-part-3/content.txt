                            <h1 class="title-post" itemprop="name">Agentic AI challenges in IDE: Forensic and extraction of traces PART 3</h1>
                    <div class="date">
            <span itemprop="datePublished"><a href="https://threat.boutique/archive/2025-12" title="Show all posts made on this month">21 December 2025</a></span>
            - Posted in            <span itemprop="articleSection"><a href="https://threat.boutique/category/incident-response">Incident Response</a></span> by            <span itemprop="author"><a href="https://threat.boutique/author/b3vSclAuunBMsdt">Jok3r</a></span> -
            <span><a href="https://threat.boutique/2025/12/agentic-ai-challenges-in-ide-forensic-and-extraction-of-traces-part-3" rel="permalink">Permalink</a></span>
        </div>
                    <div class="featured-image">
                <a href="https://threat.boutique/2025/12/agentic-ai-challenges-in-ide-forensic-and-extraction-of-traces-part-3"><img src="https://threat.boutique/content/images/20251221223938-6933.jpg" alt="Agentic AI challenges in IDE: Forensic and extraction of traces PART 3"/></a>
            </div>
                                        <div class="post-body post-1766342236" itemprop="articleBody">
            <p>Background:
In our latest research related to forensic traces left by IDEs, let's look into the Cursor AI IDE.</p>

<p>Traces:
As in our previous research, we saw that some of its components rely on VS Code. And as in previous cases, it is leaving its generated files in APPDATA directory.
<img src="https://threat.boutique/content/images/20251221224819-file_repoo.png" alt="enter image description here" /></p>

<p>Another metadata finding during forensics: we can see metadata about projects on which work had been done by the victim.
<img src="https://threat.boutique/content/images/20251221224538-project_history.png" alt="enter image description here" /></p>

<p>Conclusion:
All 3 IDEs - VS Code, Antigravity, and Cursor AI - have shown the same traces. Both Antigravity and Cursor AI have the same dependency on VS Code. According to findings at this stage, if for some reason infostealers or malware appear in the victim's environment, they can enumerate those directories and steal source code-related data.</p>
