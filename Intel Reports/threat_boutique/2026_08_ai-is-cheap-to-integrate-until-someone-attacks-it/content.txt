                            <h1 class="title-post" itemprop="name">The Incident Cost of Integrating AI into Products</h1>
                    <div class="date">
            <span itemprop="datePublished"><a href="https://threat.boutique/archive/2026-08" title="Show all posts made on this month">15 August 2026</a></span>
            - Posted in            <span itemprop="articleSection"><a href="https://threat.boutique/category/threat-analyze">Threat Analyze</a></span> by            <span itemprop="author"><a href="https://threat.boutique/author/b3vSclAuunBMsdt">Jok3r</a></span> -
            <span><a href="https://threat.boutique/2026/08/ai-is-cheap-to-integrate-until-someone-attacks-it" rel="permalink">Permalink</a></span>
        </div>
                    <div class="featured-image">
                <a href="https://threat.boutique/2026/08/ai-is-cheap-to-integrate-until-someone-attacks-it"><img src="https://threat.boutique/content/images/20260815123157-q75ej.jpg" alt="The Incident Cost of Integrating AI into Products"/></a>
            </div>
                                        <div class="post-body post-1786782206" itemprop="articleBody">
            <p><strong><em>Background:</em></strong>
Nowadays, a lot of products are integrating AI into customer support and other inner circles of their product. But have you ever thought about one challenge with this integration? Instead of the previous challenges which we already discussed, there is an existing new challenge called cost and resource consumption, which can impact availability too. 
So what does it mean for business ?
High bills and availability problems.</p>

<p><strong><em>Attack:</em></strong>
In the scenario, the attacker is making mass requests to the AI component, which is impacting the AI token price bill and resource consumption.</p>

<p><img src="https://threat.boutique/content/images/20260815124046-123.png" alt="enter image description here" /></p>

<p><strong><em>Prevention:</em></strong>
To prevent resource consumption, always limit the input data. As a source of truth, take your quarterly maximum usage of input length and token consumption.</p>

<p>Disallow mass requests per second, again taking quarterly connection requests into account.
Put a guardrail price on token usage.</p>

<p>Example of prevention
<img src="https://threat.boutique/content/images/20260815125239-Screenshot_20260815_125144.png" alt="enter image description here" /></p>

<p><img src="https://threat.boutique/content/images/20260815125335-Screenshot_20260815_125255.png" alt="enter image description here" /></p>

<p><strong><em>Conclusion:</em></strong>
The attack surface has changed with AI integration. Previously, during a DDoS attack, attackers tried to impact your availability. Now they can also leverage request volume to impact your business income. So you should review all your attack surfaces again against components where AI is involved.</p>
